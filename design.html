HUSA Karaoke Night Social Poster

This is a poster I designed for the HUSA Karaoke Night Social event. I used Adobe Illustrator to create the design, incorporating vibrant colors and playful typography to capture the fun and lively atmosphere of the event.

Dungeons&Dragons Inspired Derby Helmets

Recently, I got into Dungeons & Dragons and roller derby, so I thought "Why not combine the two?"

I transfered the on-paper sketches onto Canva in order to work with vector-based shapes. I also made a revised version, experimenting with gradients.

From top left to top right: Goblin, Unicorn, and Direwolf. From bottom left to bottom right: Mimic, Owlbear, and Beholder.
