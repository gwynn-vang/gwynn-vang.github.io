HUSA Karaoke Night Social Poster

This is a poster I designed for the HUSA Karaoke Night Social event as the marketing manager. I used Canva to create the design, incorporating vibrant colors and playful typography to capture the fun and lively atmosphere of the event.

Dungeons&Dragons Inspired Derby Helmets

Recently, I got into Dungeons & Dragons and roller derby, so I thought "Why not combine the two?"

I transfered the on-paper sketches onto Canva in order to work with vector-based shapes. I also made a revised version, experimenting with gradients. This project encouraged me to explore the intersection of fantasy and sports culture.

From top left to top right: Goblin, Unicorn, and Direwolf. From bottom left to bottom right: Mimic, Owlbear, and Beholder.

Invitation Card

This project is a simple, playful invitation card.

The first version had little to no direction toward a theme. The illustrations were directly traced from my own on-paper sketch.

The revised version is themed for birthday celebrations. This card challenged my ability to find creative designs that related to birthdays, while implementing the art into modular shapes. I found that researching a variety of cultural birthday traditions provided valuable inspiration for the design.

Make Room Album Inspiration - DIMA 323

This album cover was inspired by "Make Room" by Destroy Boys, a rock band. I used PicsArt to edit and design this image.

For this design, I took the concept of "making room" and translated it into visual elements that represents the idea of finding a way to take space in a crowded area.

I was also inspired by comic book art and the use of bold, contrasting colors.

MWMO Website Wireframes

These website mockups were made in partnership with the Mississippi Watershed Management Organization for the Digital Media Arts capstone course. This slideshow represents the requested Water Quality Monitoring landing webpage in drafts.

These wireframes were made to fulfill the clients need for a new Water Monitoring landing page layout, as well as a secondary landing page. I made wireframes alongside the Web Design team that consisted of my classmates. To make these design locally, I used Inkscape, a free vector-based illustration program.

Through this project, I learned how to adhere to a brand design and produce content that implemented quick feedback. I learned how to work with a production team and advocate for my designs.

All copytext was provided by the MWMO. All images were provided by the DIMA 480 photography team.
